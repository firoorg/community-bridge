# Firo community bridge

The Firo Core Team operates a two-way bridge between the [public Firo Telegram community](https://t.me/firoorg) and configured public channels in the [official Firo Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/TGZPRbRT3Y).

**Messages posted in a bridged space can be copied to the other platform, together with displayed sender attribution and supported media.** People on the other platform can read, save and share those posts.

- [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})
- [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }})

There is currently no per-user forwarding opt-out. For a privacy question or to request deletion of a relayed post or data under our control, contact [team@firo.org](mailto:team@firo.org).
