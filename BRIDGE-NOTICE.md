# Notice for the bridged communities

Post the following notice prominently in Telegram and every bridged Discord channel. Use the repository policy links until GitHub Pages is live, then replace them with the published Pages links.

## Copy-ready notice

**This is a public bridged chat.**

Messages posted here are forwarded between Firo's public Telegram community (@firoorg) and the linked public channels in the official Firo Discord server. Your message, displayed name and supported attachments may appear on the other platform, where people can read, save and share them.

There is currently no per-user forwarding opt-out. If you do not want a future message relayed, do not post it in this bridged chat. Deleting a message here may not automatically remove the forwarded copy.

For a privacy question or a manual deletion request, email **team@firo.org** or contact an official moderator.

- Privacy Policy: https://github.com/firoorg/community-bridge/blob/main/docs/privacy.md
- Terms of Service: https://github.com/firoorg/community-bridge/blob/main/docs/terms.md

## Before forwarding after introducing the notice

Give existing users clear notice too. Discord's Developer Terms allow sharing API data with an external recipient at a user's express direction, among the stated exceptions. Do not assume that posting the policy alone establishes that direction.

Use an appropriate affirmative acknowledgement of cross-platform forwarding before relaying a user's Discord posts, such as a clearly explained channel-access acknowledgement. Keep sufficient evidence of the direction. An acknowledgement should identify Telegram as the destination and explain what is forwarded. This repository does not implement that control or change the live bot.
