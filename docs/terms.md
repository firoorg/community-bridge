# Firo community bridge terms of service

Effective date: 19 October 2026  
Last updated: 19 October 2026

## 1. Service and operator

The **Firo Core Team**, an unincorporated group, operates the Firo community bridge using TediCross. The bridge connects the [public Firo Telegram community at @firoorg](https://t.me/firoorg) with configured public channels in the [official Firo Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/TGZPRbRT3Y).

These terms apply to use of the bridge. Questions, complaints, privacy requests and content-removal requests can be sent to **[team@firo.org](mailto:team@firo.org)**.

Discord's and Telegram's own terms and policies also apply to use of their services. These bridge terms do not override either platform's rules or applicable law.

## 2. Cross-platform forwarding

The bridge operates in both directions. Posts in linked spaces can be copied to the other platform, together with displayed sender attribution and supported attachments or reply context. People on the destination platform can read, save, quote and redistribute those posts.

When you knowingly choose to submit a post for relay in a space identified as bridged, you direct us to deliver it to the linked public destination. You retain your rights in your content and give the permission necessary to copy, format and deliver the post for that purpose. This permission is limited to providing the relay and handling its necessary technical copies.

This clause does not treat merely joining a server as authorization to share everything you post, or treat earlier undisclosed forwarding as retroactively authorized. Applicable notice and user-authorization requirements continue to apply.

If you do not want a future post forwarded, do not submit it in a bridged space. There is currently no per-user forwarding opt-out. Contact us if you need help identifying linked spaces or asking about alternatives.

## 3. Eligibility and acceptable use

You must meet the relevant platform's age requirements and applicable law, and follow the rules of the linked Firo communities.

Share only content you are entitled to make available to the linked public audience. Do not use the bridge for unlawful activity, scams, impersonation, harassment, doxxing, malware, spam, infringement of others' rights, disruption of the service or evasion of moderation.

Do not publish another person's confidential or private information without appropriate authorization.

## 4. Moderation

Authorized moderators may remove posts, prevent particular content from being relayed or restrict access to enforce community and platform rules, protect users or address service problems.

Moderation on one platform may require separate action on the other. We do not guarantee that every message is reviewed before forwarding or that a moderation action automatically propagates between services.

## 5. Delivery, edits and deletion

The bridge is provided as available. Messages can be delayed, duplicated, reformatted or interrupted, and some media and formatting may not be supported. Features depend on the software, its configuration and the platforms' APIs.

Edits and deletions propagate only where supported. Removing an original message may leave a forwarded copy visible. Moderators can handle removal requests manually; contact **[team@firo.org](mailto:team@firo.org)** or an official community moderator and identify the relevant posts.

We handle requests concerning posts and records within our possession or control in accordance with the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) and applicable obligations. We cannot guarantee removal of copies independently saved by participants or information controlled solely by a platform.
